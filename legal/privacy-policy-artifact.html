This policy describes how Credify Health Inc. ("Credify Health," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our internal credentialing and invoicing tools, including our integration with QuickBooks Online.

§1

Information We Collect

  • Provider and enrollment data — provider names, NPI numbers, license information, and payer/insurance enrollment details, collected as part of our credentialing services.
  • Client and billing data — practice/client names, invoice amounts, deposit and balance payment status, and related billing records.
  • Account data — information necessary to connect to QuickBooks Online via OAuth, including access tokens used solely to read and write invoice and payment data on your behalf.
§2

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to operate our internal credentialing workflow: tracking enrollment status, calculating fees, generating invoices in QuickBooks Online, and reconciling payment status. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

§3

How We Share Information

Information is shared only with the third-party services necessary to provide this functionality — currently ClickUp (task and workflow tracking) and Intuit QuickBooks Online (invoicing and payment records) — under each provider's own terms and security practices. We do not otherwise share this data with third parties.

§4

Data Retention & Security

Data is retained for as long as needed to support active credentialing and billing records, and is stored using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity.

§5

Your Rights

Individuals whose information appears in our credentialing records may contact us using the information below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their information, subject to our recordkeeping obligations.
